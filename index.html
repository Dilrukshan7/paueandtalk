About Pause & Talk

Built around one idea: everyone deserves to be heard.

About photo

Our story

Pause & Talk started as a simple observation: most people don't need a solution, they need someone to listen. Not a friend who's too close to the situation, not a professional running the clock on a treatment plan. Just a calm, attentive presence, for exactly as long as they need it.

Mission

To give people a private space to speak freely, without judgment, obligation, or the pressure to be okay.

Vision

A world where reaching out for a conversation feels as normal as reaching out for anything else.

Why we exist

Not everyone needs therapy, but almost everyone needs to be heard.

Talking to someone impartial can be easier than talking to those closest to us.

Support shouldn't require a diagnosis or a waitlist.

Core values

Confidentiality

What's shared stays private, always.

Warmth

Every conversation starts with care.

Presence

Full attention, for the whole session.

Respect

You lead; we follow.

Our safe space commitment

Every session is private, unrecorded, and free of judgment. We listen without trying to fix, diagnose, or direct. You lead the conversation.

Privacy & Trust

Your privacy is the foundation, not an afterthought.

Privacy commitment

Every conversation is private. We never share what you tell us with anyone, for any reason, without your explicit consent.

Confidentiality

Sessions aren't therapy, but we hold them to the same standard of discretion. What's said in a session stays there.

Data handling

We collect only what's needed to schedule and run a session — your contact details and preferred times. We don't sell or share your data with third parties.

Safe conversations

Sessions are never recorded. There's no transcript, no audio file, and no chat log kept beyond what's needed to remember context you've asked us to.

Code of ethics

We listen without judgment. We don't diagnose or treat. We refer out when something is beyond our scope. We respect boundaries, always.

Important: Pause & Talk is a listening and companionship service — not therapy, counseling, or crisis intervention. If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline immediately.
